Construction Law.

From the first tender to the final handover — clear, commercially-minded counsel for every stage of a construction project.

Overview

Construction is one of the firm’s founding practices. We represent developers, contractors, subcontractors and property owners across the full life of a project — negotiating and drafting contracts, managing tenders, and advising on planning and zoning.

When disputes arise, we act decisively — building defects, delays, liens and payment claims — always weighing the commercial outcome, not only the legal one.

  • 02Tenders and procurement
  • 03Planning & zoning
